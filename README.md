Technical Techno Tech (TTT)

Project Overview

Technical Techno Tech (TTT) is a hands-on IT infrastructure and service management lab designed to simulate the IT operations of a fictional enterprise. The project combines infrastructure administration, troubleshooting, networking, Microsoft technologies, and ITIL 4 service management practices.

Current Status

This repository is currently undergoing restructuring.

The project is being reorganized to establish a more structured, service-oriented approach to IT operations. This includes documenting infrastructure, formalizing service management procedures, developing reusable templates, and aligning technical activities with ITIL 4 practices.

During this transition, some documentation and workflows may be incomplete or subject to change.

Archives

Previous lab projects and technical exercises are preserved in the Archives/ directory.

These archived projects document earlier work, including Windows Server administration, Active Directory, and related infrastructure exercises. They provide a record of completed work and a foundation for future improvements.

As the project evolves, selected archived exercises may be revisited and rebuilt using the updated service management framework.

Repository Structure

The repository is organized around the following areas:

* Infrastructure — Technical architecture, networking, virtualization, Windows Server, and directory services.
* Service Management — Procedures and documentation supporting incident management, service requests, problem management, change enablement, and related ITIL practices.
* Service Configuration Management — Configuration item records, infrastructure relationships, and the configuration item register.
* Knowledge Management — Knowledge articles and documentation lifecycle practices.
* Service Desk — Ticket intake, categorization, prioritization, and escalation procedures.
* Templates — Reusable formats for documenting incidents, service requests, changes, configuration items, and other service management records.
* Scenarios — Planned and developing scenarios for practical troubleshooting and service management exercises.
* Archives — Previous projects and lab exercises retained for reference.

Project Objectives

* Develop practical IT infrastructure and troubleshooting skills.
* Document infrastructure configurations and dependencies.
* Apply ITIL 4 service management practices to realistic technical scenarios.
* Establish repeatable procedures and consistent documentation.
* Build a foundation for future automation and AI-assisted IT operations.

Important Note

TTT is a fictional company used to structure a realistic technical lab. The infrastructure and configurations may be real lab resources, while business activities, employee scenarios, and service requests may be simulated.

The repository will continue to evolve as archived projects are reviewed and the updated environment is developed.
