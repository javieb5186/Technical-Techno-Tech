# SOP-KM-001 - Knowledge Article Lifecycle Management

## Purpose

This procedure defines how knowledge articles are created, maintained, and retired within Technical Techno Tech.

The objective is to ensure technical knowledge is:

- Accurate
- Reusable
- Accessible
- Consistently documented

## Knowledge Article Lifecycle

```text
Knowledge Opportunity
        ↓
Article Creation
        ↓
Technical Review
        ↓
Publication
        ↓
Operational Use
        ↓
Review and Updates
        ↓
Retirement
```

## Knowledge Article Creation Process

### Step 1 - Identify a Knowledge Opportunity

Knowledge articles may be created from:

- Service Requests
- Incidents
- Lessons Learned

### Step 2 - Create the Article

Assign the next available KB number.

Example:

```text
KB-001 New Employee Onboarding
KB-002 Force Entra Connect Synchronization
KB-003 Reset Active Directory Password
```

### Step 3 - Document Technical Knowledge

Include only information that is:

- Accurate
- Relevant
- Reusable
- Appropriate for the intended audience

### Step 4 - Validate Content

Review:

- Technical accuracy
- Commands
- Procedures
- References
- Screenshots

### Step 5 - Publish

Store the article in the appropriate Knowledge Base category.

## Knowledge Article Standards

All articles should contain:

- Overview
- Purpose
- Requirements
- Guidance or Procedure
- Verification / Validation
- Common Issues
- Related Documentation
- Skills Practiced

Additional sections may be added as needed.

## Maintenance

Knowledge articles should be updated when:

- Technical procedures change
- Infrastructure changes
- New requirements are introduced
- Documentation errors are identified

## Retirement

Articles should be retired when:

- Technology is replaced
- Procedures are obsolete
- A newer article supersedes the content

Retired articles should remain available for historical reference when appropriate.

## Related Documentation

### Service Request Management

Service Requests may reference Knowledge Articles used during fulfillment.

### Infrastructure Documentation

Knowledge Articles should align with current infrastructure documentation.

### Scenarios

Operational scenarios should reference applicable Knowledge Articles.
