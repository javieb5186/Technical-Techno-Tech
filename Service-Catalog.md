# Technical Techno Tech — IT Service Catalog

## Overview

**Organization:** Technical Techno Tech (TTT)  
**Service Provider:** Internal IT Department  
**Service Consumers:** Technical Techno Tech Employees and Authorized Personnel  
**Service Management Framework:** ITIL-Aligned Practices  
**Service Management Platform:** Jira Service Management

## Purpose

The Technical Techno Tech IT Service Catalog provides a centralized overview of the internal IT services available to employees and authorized personnel.

The catalog defines the services provided by the Internal IT Department, their intended purpose, and the types of assistance employees can request.

It also supports consistent service delivery, request fulfillment, technical support, and IT service management.

This catalog covers **internal IT services only**. External customer services, such as software development, website development, and game development, are outside its scope.

## Request Submission & Fulfillment

Employees and authorized personnel may request IT services through approved support channels.

### Request Channels

- Jira Service Management employee portal
- Authorized email requests
- Direct contact with the IT Service Desk

### General Request Workflow

1. **Request Submission:** An employee or authorized requester submits an IT service request.
2. **Request Classification:** The Service Desk identifies the requested service and appropriate request type.
3. **Verification & Authorization:** IT verifies the requester's identity and obtains any required approvals.
4. **Request Fulfillment:** The responsible IT personnel perform the requested activity.
5. **Validation:** IT verifies that the request was completed successfully.
6. **Documentation & Closure:** The request is documented and closed according to established procedures.

Specific fulfillment steps depend on the service and request type.

## Incident Reporting

The Service Catalog describes available IT services and request offerings.

Unexpected interruptions, failures, or degradation of those services are handled through Incident Management.

Requests and incidents may involve the same technology but require different management workflows.

## ITIL Management Practice Alignment

The Service Catalog identifies what IT services are available.

ITIL-aligned management practices define how those services are supported, controlled, and improved.

| Management Practice | Relationship to IT Services |
|---|---|
| Service Desk | Provides the central point of contact for users. |
| Incident Management | Restores normal service operation following incidents. |
| Service Request Management | Manages and fulfills predefined user requests. |
| Knowledge Management | Maintains reusable service and troubleshooting information. |
| Problem Management | Investigates underlying causes of incidents. |
| Change Enablement | Assesses and authorizes changes to IT services and infrastructure. |
| Monitoring and Event Management | Observes service and infrastructure conditions. |
| Service Configuration Management | Maintains information about configuration items supporting services. |
| Information Security Management | Protects information and supports security requirements. |
| Continual Improvement | Identifies and implements service improvements. |

## Service Catalog Maintenance

The Service Catalog should be reviewed and updated when:

- A new internal IT service becomes available.
- An existing service is modified or retired.
- New technologies are implemented.
- Service ownership or responsibilities change.
- Request offerings or approval requirements change.
- Improvements are identified through IT service management activities.

Service descriptions should reflect the capabilities actually available within the emulated environment.
