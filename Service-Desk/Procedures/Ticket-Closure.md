## SOP — Ticket Closure

**Organization:** Technical Techno Tech (TTT)  
**Department:** Internal IT / Service Desk  
**Document Type:** Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)  
**Procedure ID:** SOP-SD-005  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Procedure  
**ITIL Practices:** Service Desk, Incident Management, Service Request Management, Knowledge Management

# 1. Purpose

This procedure establishes a standardized process for closing IT support tickets within Technical Techno Tech.

Ticket closure ensures that:

- Required work has been completed.
- Requested services have been fulfilled.
- Incidents have been resolved.
- Documentation is complete.
- Knowledge has been retained.
- Requesters are appropriately informed.
- Accurate records are maintained.

**Key Principle:** A ticket should not be closed simply because technical work has ended. A ticket should only be closed after fulfillment, validation, documentation, and communication requirements have been completed.

# 2. Scope

This procedure applies to:

- Incidents
- Service Requests
- Security-related tickets
- General inquiries
- Escalated tickets returned for closure

# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

## Service Desk Technician

Responsible for:

- Reviewing completed work
- Verifying ticket requirements
- Recording work notes
- Recording resolution information
- Updating ticket status
- Communicating with the requester
- Identifying knowledge article opportunities
- Closing tickets

## Technical Support Personnel

Responsible for:

- Providing resolution details
- Recording technical findings
- Validating technical outcomes
- Returning tickets for appropriate closure

## Requester

Responsible for:

- Confirming service restoration when appropriate
- Reporting unresolved issues
- Providing clarification if required

**Lab Note:** These roles are emulated. A single learner may perform multiple roles during an exercise.

# 4. Closure Requirements

Before a ticket may be closed, the following conditions should be satisfied.

## Required

- Requested work completed or issue resolved
- Validation performed
- Work notes documented
- Resolution documented
- Time tracking updated (if applicable)
- Ticket status updated

## When Applicable

- Requester notified
- Knowledge article reviewed or created
- Escalation records completed
- Related records linked
- Screenshots or evidence attached

# 5. Ticket Closure Procedure

## Step 1 — Review Ticket

Review:

- Summary
- Description
- Category
- Priority
- Assigned personnel
- Work notes
- Resolution notes

**Expected Result:** Technician understands the completed work and current ticket status.

---

## Step 2 — Validate Completion

Confirm that the requested action or technical resolution was completed.

Examples:

### Incident

- Service restored
- User can access resource
- Application functions correctly

### Service Request

- Account created
- Access assigned
- License assigned
- Software installed

**Expected Result:** Technical work is completed.

---

## Step 3 — Perform Verification

Verify the outcome using available methods.

Examples:

- User account exists
- Group membership assigned
- Share permissions configured
- Synchronization completed
- Application launches successfully

Verification should be documented.

**Expected Result:** The technician confirms that the requested outcome was achieved.

---

## Step 4 — Document Work Notes

Record significant actions performed during ticket handling.

Example:

- User account created
- Added to Finance_Users
- Added to Finance_Share_RW
- Entra ID synchronization performed
- Share permissions verified

Work notes should be factual and chronological.

**Expected Result:** Future reviewers understand what actions were completed.

---

## Step 5 — Document Resolution

Record the final outcome.

Examples:

### Incident

Resolution:
User restored to Finance_Share_RW group membership.

### Service Request

Resolution:
New employee onboarding completed successfully.

Resolution notes should summarize:

- What was completed
- What was fixed
- What was provided

**Expected Result:** Resolution is clearly documented.

---

## Step 6 — Review Knowledge Requirements

Determine whether:

- An existing knowledge article was used.
- An existing knowledge article requires updating.
- A new knowledge article should be created.

Create or update knowledge documentation when new information was discovered.

**Expected Result:** Organizational knowledge is retained.

---

## Step 7 — Notify Requester

Provide a closure update.

Typical communication should include:

- Ticket reference
- Completion confirmation
- Summary of work performed
- Any follow-up requirements

**Expected Result:** Requester is informed of the outcome.

---

## Step 8 — Record Time

Update:

- Time spent
- Remaining estimate
- Final effort

if these fields are used within the ticketing platform.

**Expected Result:** Effort tracking is complete.

---

## Step 9 — Update Ticket Status

Change ticket status to:

- Resolved
- Fulfilled
- Closed

according to the applicable workflow.

Use the appropriate resolution code.

Examples:

- Fulfilled
- Resolved
- Completed
- Cancelled
- Duplicate

**Expected Result:** Ticket accurately reflects its final state.

# 6. Closure Checklist

Before closing a ticket verify:

- [ ] Work completed
- [ ] Validation performed
- [ ] Work notes recorded
- [ ] Resolution documented
- [ ] Knowledge reviewed
- [ ] Requester notified
- [ ] Time recorded
- [ ] Status updated
- [ ] Appropriate resolution selected

# 7. Common Closure Errors

## Error 1 — Closing Without Validation

Incorrect:

Work completed but not verified.

Correct:

Verify that the desired outcome was achieved.

---

## Error 2 — Missing Work Notes

Incorrect:

Ticket closed with no activity record.

Correct:

Document major actions performed.

---

## Error 3 — Missing Resolution

Incorrect:

"Fixed."

Correct:

Document what was fixed and how.

---

## Error 4 — Failing To Capture Knowledge

Incorrect:

Discovering a new solution and not documenting it.

Correct:

Create or update a knowledge article.

---

## Error 5 — Closing Before Communication

Incorrect:

Closing the ticket without notifying the requester.

Correct:

Provide a closure update when appropriate.
